Create a modern, premium, energetic website for a sports and skills academy called **FunSkill**.

### Brand

**Name:** FunSkill
**Tagline:** **Learn. Play. Master.**
**Business:** A multi-skill academy that teaches children and young people **Chess and Roller Skating**, with the possibility to expand into other sports and skills later.

FunSkill should feel like a professional international academy — fun, trustworthy, youthful, competitive, and family-friendly. Do NOT copy the visual design, text, logo, or layout of any existing company. Create an original identity inspired by the idea of a premium multi-sport academy.

### Target audience

* Parents looking for quality activities for their children
* Children and teenagers
* Schools looking for extracurricular/co-curricular programs
* Organizations looking for sports and skills training

### Main navigation

Home | Programs | Chess | Skating | Schools | About Us | Coaches | Locations | Contact

Add a prominent **Book a Trial** button in the navigation.

### Homepage

Create a visually impressive homepage with:

1. **Hero section**

* Large headline: **"Turn Skills Into Confidence."**
* Supporting text: "FunSkill helps children learn, compete and grow through Chess and Roller Skating."
* Primary CTA: **Book a Free Trial**
* Secondary CTA: **Explore Programs**
* Use energetic, high-quality imagery showing children playing chess and roller skating.
* Include subtle motion/animation effects.

2. **Two core programs**
   Create two large interactive cards:

**CHESS**
"Think. Strategize. Win."

* Beginner to advanced
* Critical thinking
* Strategy
* Competition
* Age-based training
* CTA: Explore Chess

**ROLLER SKATING**
"Balance. Move. Fly."

* Beginner to advanced
* Balance & coordination
* Fitness
* Confidence
* Competition
* CTA: Explore Skating

3. **Why FunSkill**
   Show 4–6 benefits:

* Professional Coaches
* Safe Learning Environment
* Beginner to Advanced
* Confidence Building
* Competitive Opportunities
* Fun & Engaging Training

4. **How FunSkill Works**
   Create a simple 4-step journey:

5. Choose a Skill

6. Book a Trial

7. Train With Coaches

8. Grow & Compete

9. **Programs section**
   Show programs by age:

* Tiny Champions — Ages 4–6
* Young Explorers — Ages 7–10
* Rising Stars — Ages 11–14
* Future Champions — Ages 15+

Allow users to filter between Chess and Skating.

6. **Schools & Organizations**
   Create a dedicated section:
   **"Bring FunSkill to Your School"**
   Explain that FunSkill can provide structured Chess and Roller Skating programs for schools and organizations.
   CTA: **Partner With FunSkill**

7. **Coaches**
   Show professional coach cards with:

* Photo
* Name
* Specialty
* Experience
* Certifications

8. **Success / statistics section**
   Use animated counters such as:

* 1,000+ Students
* 25+ Coaches
* 50+ Schools
* 10+ Locations

Make these editable placeholders rather than claiming they are real.

9. **Testimonials**
   Create parent/student testimonial cards with realistic placeholder content.

10. **Locations**
    Create an interactive-looking location section with:

* Search location
* City cards
* "Find a FunSkill Academy Near You"

11. **Final CTA**
    Large energetic section:
    **"Ready to Discover Your Skill?"**
    "Start your FunSkill journey today."
    Buttons:
    **Book a Free Trial**
    **Contact Us**

12. **Footer**
    Include:

* FunSkill logo
* Short company description
* Programs
* Company
* Schools
* Contact
* Social media icons
* Newsletter signup
* Privacy Policy
* Terms & Conditions

### Visual identity

Create an original premium sports-academy visual system.

Use:

* Bright, energetic primary color
* Dark navy/black for contrast
* White/light backgrounds
* One secondary accent color
* Large bold typography
* Rounded cards
* Soft shadows
* Dynamic diagonal/curved shapes
* Sport-inspired graphic elements
* High-quality photography
* Subtle gradients

The design should feel:
**Premium + Fun + Athletic + Educational + International**

Avoid making it look like a traditional school website.

### Logo

Create a simple modern **FunSkill** wordmark/logo concept.

The logo should communicate:
**Fun + Learning + Movement + Growth**

Create a small recognizable symbol that can work as:

* Website logo
* Mobile app icon
* Social media profile
* Sports uniform
* Cap/shirt branding

### UX requirements

Make the website fully responsive for:

* Desktop
* Tablet
* Mobile

Use clear CTA hierarchy.

Important CTAs:
**Book a Free Trial**
**Explore Programs**
**Partner With FunSkill**

Create hover states, buttons, cards, navigation states and smooth transitions.

### Figma Make requirements

Build this as a complete clickable website prototype, not just a static landing page.

Create reusable components for:

* Navbar
* Buttons
* Program cards
* Coach cards
* Testimonial cards
* Location cards
* Footer
* Forms
* Modals

Create these pages:

1. Home
2. Programs
3. Chess
4. Roller Skating
5. Schools
6. About
7. Coaches
8. Locations
9. Contact
10. Book a Trial

Include realistic placeholder content and images, but make all statistics, names, locations and contact details clearly editable.

The final result should look like a **real international sports academy brand ready to launch**, not a generic AI-generated template.
